Every quick PDF fix, in one app.

Nine everyday tasks — just switch tabs.

Merge, split, extract

Combine, divide, or keep just the pages you need.

  • Merge: any number of files, in the order you set
  • Split: every page on its own, or by page ranges
  • Extract: pages such as "1-5, 10" into one PDF

Watermark

Put text such as CONFIDENTIAL on every page.

  • Text, size, opacity and angle
  • Gray, red or blue
  • Japanese text works, too

Page numbers, header & footer

Add numbers, dates or a title to every page.

  • Page numbers at the top or bottom, left / center / right, in four formats (1, 1 / N, - 1 -, Page 1)
  • Headers and footers with [page], [pages] and [date]
  • Start number and font size

Password

Set a password to open the file, and one for editing.

  • Open password and owner password
  • Choose whether to allow printing and copying
  • 128-bit encryption

Image ↔ PDF

Turn pages into images, and images into a PDF.

  • PDF → image: PNG or JPEG at 72, 150 or 300 dpi, for the pages you choose
  • Image → PDF: any number of images in one PDF (image size, A4 portrait or A4 landscape)
  • Opens PNG, JPEG, BMP, GIF and TIFF

Text extraction

Save the text of a PDF as a text file.

  • All pages, or a page range
  • A heading for each page

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a watermark such as CONFIDENTIAL to every page?

Yes. In the Watermark tab, set the text, size, opacity, angle and color (gray, red or blue), and it is added to every page.

Can I combine many PDFs into one?

Yes. Add them to Merge PDF, set the order with Up / Down, and save.

Can I take out just the pages I need?

Yes. In Extract Pages, enter pages such as "1-5, 10, 15-20" and they are saved together as one PDF.

Can I protect a PDF with a password?

Yes. Set an open password, an owner password, or both, and choose whether to allow printing and copying.

Can I turn PDF pages into images?

Yes. Image ↔ PDF exports pages as PNG or JPEG at 72, 150 or 300 dpi.

Can I combine phone photos into one PDF?

Yes. Add them under Image → PDF and choose a page size such as A4 portrait; each photo is centered on its own A4 page.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. All PDF processing happens on your PC.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. Buy it once on the Microsoft Store and keep using it with no monthly fee.
